About Steven.

Engineer. Writer. Storyteller. Husband, father, and friend.

Steven Schrembeck

Steven Schrembeck was a UI developer and Principal Engineer at Cox Automotive. He was Amanda’s husband and a father to their two sons. He passed away in September 2026.

His creative work ranged across essays, podcasts, audio fiction, and video. A Different Compass, Reality 101, Collected, and Seeking Minima are different ways into his interests and imagination.

A home for the work.

This archive brings together saved copies of the things he made and shared, alongside links to their original sources. It is for his family, for the people who knew him, and for anyone who finds something here worth spending time with.
