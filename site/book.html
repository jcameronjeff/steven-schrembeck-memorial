About this edition

The Small Moments Are Everything brings together selected passages from Steven Schrembeck’s recorded speech. It moves from ordinary life and work through attention, learning, and making, then into his accounts of consciousness, telepathy, and the nature of reality.

The sentences, vocabulary, humor, and claims in the chapters come from the source transcripts. Filler, false starts, repeated explanations, recording logistics, and selected passages have been cut. Punctuation, capitalization, and paragraph breaks have been edited. Passages have been arranged into chapters. His own qualifications and expressions of uncertainty remain alongside his convictions.
