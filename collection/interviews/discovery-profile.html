About Steven Schrembeck

Reality is far more mysterious and loving than we’ve been led to believe

I keep an online journal of impossible experiences called "Reality 101", here: https://reality101.captivate.fm/

I keep an online journal of impossible experiences called "Reality 101", here: https://reality101.captivate.fm/

In 2022, a series of UFO sightings forced me to re-evaluate almost everything I thought I knew about reality.

I was a logical, materialist, atheist software engineer. I liked systems, measurable evidence, and rule-based explanations. Then things started happening that did not fit the model. Far, far beyond just UFO sightings.

I've spent the last few years obsessively exploring consciousness through meditation, remote viewing, psychic powers, channeling, out of body experiences in order to answer the kinds of questions most people never actually expect answers to:

Are we alone?
Who and what am I, and why am I here?
Do we die?
What is consciousness?
How does reality actually work?

I enjoy sharing what I've experience and learned, what I believe it means, and walking through how I got to those beliefs so that the audience can decide what it means for themselves. Most importantly, I'll share everything I can about how to repeat and validate these ideas on their own. I stand by the belief that, if any of this is true, it should be directly exploreable.

What I have to share is first-hand stories and learned experience. That, and the analysis I can construe from that experience
