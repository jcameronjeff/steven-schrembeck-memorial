## Recordings not represented

The prologue was first published on Joshua Swenson's podcast *Ruptures and Roots* as "Beyond Logic: Steven Schrembeck on Mind Sight, Soul Families, and the Edges of Consciousness." The archive holds that recording too. It is the same conversation and appears here once.

Steven's 2023 interview on *The CTO Show with Mehmet Gonullu* concerns building products with AI and is not represented. Two further guest appearances listed on his speaking profile, on *The Creative Mindset* and *The Podcast Inside Your House*, could not be recovered for the archive. His audio fiction, his videos and his workplace talks are outside the scope of this book.

## Small corrections

The chapters print what the speakers said, including a few slips of memory. For the record: the telekinesis manual Steven calls *The Telekinesis Practice Method* is Sean McNamara's *Defy Your Limits: The Telekinesis Training Method*. The history of levitation Joshua mentions, Carlos Eire's *They Flew*, concerns the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries. In the study of Wim Hof's method, volunteers were injected with a bacterial toxin, not with pathogens. In the rubber hand experiment that Steven asks about, a threat to the rubber hand produces an anxiety response; the published study does not report pain. And "the law of confusion," in the *Law of One* books he takes it from, is a name for free will.

## Dates

Dates for the series are the publication dates in its podcast feed, not recording dates. The feed dates the three journals October 1 to 3, 2026. Steven's death in September 2026 is recorded in the memorial written by his friend Jeff Cameron. The epigraph is the line Steven placed at the top of his speaking profile, preserved in the archive.

## Further reading

Steven wrote about the same experiences and ideas in essays at his site *A Different Compass*. The archive preserves them, among them "Reality 102: Souls," "See," "How to know," "To Know," "Feel it first," "How to get impossible things faster," and "The Gift of Everything." A shorter compiled book, *The Small Moments Are Everything*, draws selections from across his recordings, including his talks on work and meaning.
