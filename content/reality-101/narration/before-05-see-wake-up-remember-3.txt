The most conversational chapter in the book, and Joshua earns his place in it. His own practice points somewhere else, he admits he is jealous, and he asks the question the series needs someone to ask: why should anyone try for this while they are alive?

Steven's answers are here: on aptitude, on other paths being valid, on why he wants everyone to have what he has. So is a claim worth marking for later, that each time somebody does one of these things it gets a little easier for everyone else.
