Part Three steps back. These four interviews come from before the series, and in them he is a founder being asked about technology.

I put them in because the man in Part One did not come from nowhere. This first conversation has no anomalous experience in it at all. It is about what he called the untrustworthy operator: how you rely on yourself when you know you can't. His answer is to notice what a thing does to you and then arrange your life accordingly, without waiting for willpower to show up. I think it is the same move he makes later, with much stranger material.
