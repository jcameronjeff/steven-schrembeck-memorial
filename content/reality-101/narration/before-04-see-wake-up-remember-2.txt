In the episode's description he wrote that this one is "about how it feels to work, alone, for something impossible, not knowing whether you'll ever get it."

It opens in January, back at his job, depressed, with the experiences gone. There is a fox, and his late father's voice on the night before his birthday. Then two months of effort with nothing to show for it. It ends on April 8th, in a closet and then in a hammock, with the experience he says he made the show to describe.

If you only know the confident voice of the first lecture, read this one for the other voice.
