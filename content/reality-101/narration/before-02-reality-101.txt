This is the night the series is named for. He dates it to the end of December, five months before this recording. He wakes in the state between sleeping and waking, and what follows comes in two movements: a meeting, first with a woman he calls his soul twin and then with a presence he calls Mother, and a lesson.

Two things to notice. One is how ordinary the first half is. Someone offers him cake and gets impatient with him. The other is his reaction to being shown, as he understands it, how everything works. He is disappointed. Then he makes a choice, and I think that choice is the hinge of the whole series.
