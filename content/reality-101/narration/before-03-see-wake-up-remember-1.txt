The next three chapters are one long conversation, released over two months, about what happened between the first lecture and the second. They are the part of the series where he is least sure of himself and most honest about it. Early on he warns Joshua that he tells it as a clean path so that it can be followed, and that living it was nothing like that.

This first part covers the days right after: the drive home, an offer to answer five questions, a ball of light on a night walk. Then it turns practical, to the psi wheel and to seeing with his eyes closed, and Joshua tests him on the spot by holding colored pens up to a camera. Read that test for what it is. It is informal, the lighting is bad, the results are mixed, and it settles nothing. He agrees to it without hesitating, which tells you something about him.
