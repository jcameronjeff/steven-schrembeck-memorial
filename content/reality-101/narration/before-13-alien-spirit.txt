The longest of the earlier interviews, and the one where he lets the most show: consciousness as fundamental, a meditation in which he felt what it is like to be a tree, his own kitchen trials with freezing water, and how he first came to the host, Sev, as a client.

The second half is the clearest account he gave of the work he did on the side, building tools so that people like him would not have to rely on willpower. He is frank in it about his own habits. I have left that as he said it.

It ends on the phrase I took as the motto for my pages.
