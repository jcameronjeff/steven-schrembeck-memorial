# Contact and teachers: who else has reported what Steven describes

Chapters 01, 02, 03 and 07 were read in full and the rest searched for names. Steven's words are cited as file number and block (07 B0016). Monroe's three books were read directly in scanned text. Other quotations came through a fetch tool: re-check them against the page before print. The shared search budget ran out part-way, so anything resting on memory or a single secondary source is marked UNVERIFIED.

## Strongest parallels

1. **Monroe's "nonverbal communication" (close; a shared lineage).** Steven: "I think it's best to describe telepathy as a shared mind experience" (07 B0016), carried by "thought balls" (07 B0035) and dialled by a "soul tone" that is "basically your phone number" (07 B0044). Robert Monroe's Far Journeys (1985) calls this NVC, defines "Rote: Thought ball" and "Ident: Mental name or 'address,' i.e., energy pattern of item", and writes feeling-tone as tags ("Rolled: Amused, laughed"), as Steven describes "a gesture or an emote" (07 B0039). Steven credits "thought balls" to Birdie Jaworski and Joshua names Monroe (06 B0007), so the words came down a lineage; the match in the experience goes beyond the borrowed words.

2. **Monroe's teachers (close).** Steven is quizzed in lucid dreams ("They're training my discernment", 01 B0252), taught through a staged scene ("this matrix, this simulation you dumped me in", 02 B0192) and told "Even your experience of me is you" (02 B0203). Monroe reports "sleeper's classes", a teacher who was "a brilliant white, radiating ball of light", lessons by "simulation" and guides "astoundingly patient"; in Ultimate Journey (1994) he finds his guide belonged to his own larger self ("My friend? One of you!"). Monroe travels out of body and meets no mother or twin.

3. **The FREE experiencer survey (close in kind; weak evidence).** Steven: "It's a white plain. There's a floor, but no ceiling and walls" (01 B0269); "We're not gonna abduct you" (02 B0135). Hernandez and colleagues (2018) surveyed 3,256 self-selected people and concluded that reported contact is "largely non-physical"; about a third of second-phase respondents reported repeated meetings in a "Matrix"-like "reality with no boundaries" that felt as "real as talking with a family member".

4. **Healthy voice-hearers (close on function).** Steven: "You have the ultimate control" (07 B0048); "any messaging at all, goes away when I got stressed or I didn't have sleep" (02 B0092). A Yale study (Powers, Kelley and Corlett 2017) found self-described psychics hear voices much like those of patients with psychosis but can start and stop them, are not distressed, find them kind, and attribute them to spirits. Their voices began at a mean age of about 7.5; Steven's began in his mid-thirties.

5. **Swedenborg on how spirits speak (close on mechanism; outside Steven's milieu).** Steven: "they're using your hardware and your reality" (07 B0095), so what he does not understand is hard to receive. Heaven and Hell (1758) §246: "Angels who talk with man do not talk in their own language, nor in any language unknown to man"; they join with the person's thought and memory.

6. **Writers and their characters (close; the ordinary-psychology reading, and Steven's own analogy).** Steven: "It feels like writing dialogue in a book a lot of the time" (01 B0267); Joshua: "we've both written a lot of dialogue, and it kind of feels like that" (03 B0027). Taylor and colleagues (2003) found 92% of 50 fiction writers felt their characters act on their own; Foxwell and colleagues (2020) found 61% of 181 professional writers said the same. Steven half-grants it: "seventy to ninety percent of the information is just you talking to yourself" (07 B0099).

7. **The imaginal: Corbin and Jung (close in concept).** Steven: "Imagination isn't less real. It's just real in a different way" (07 B0061). Henry Corbin (1964) coined "imaginal" for a real intermediate world, as against the "imaginary". Jung said the figure Philemon showed him "there are things in the psyche which I do not produce". Joshua brings the word "imaginal" into the conversation (01 B0027, B0045; 02 B0087) and Steven then uses it (02 B0168).

8. **Eben Alexander's three-part message (moderate).** Steven's sign-off: "Nothing is impossible, you're not going to die, and everything loves you" (03 B0473). Alexander (2012) reports being told, without speech: "You are loved and cherished, dearly, forever", "You have nothing to fear", "There is nothing you can do wrong", by a young woman he later identified as a birth sister he had never met. A coma experience, and its reliability has been publicly challenged.

## Source notes

### monroe1985
- Citation: Robert A. Monroe, Far Journeys. Garden City, NY: Doubleday, 1985.
- Confirmed at: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Monroe (publication); text read in a scan at https://archive.org/details/far_journeys (ch. 7, about pp. 78–81, and ch. 8 "Contact Point", about pp. 92–94, of the scanned paperback; cite from a print copy).
- What it reports: A radio executive whose out-of-body episodes began in 1958 describes wordless exchange with beings he calls INSPECS: "It was painfully slow on my side, astoundingly patient on theirs." He recalls "Sleeper's classes—attended by countless humans during a portion of their deep sleep", and says "A favorite quick and learn-forever method of theirs was simulation." One session is logged "Time: 2:17 A.M."
- Parallel: 07 B0035; 07 B0044 "I call it a soul tone"; 01 B0252; 01 B0269 "I wake up around 2:00 a.m."; 03 B0071 "these people are way more patient than we are." Closeness: close. Difference: Monroe reports cosmic travel; Steven stays in bed and reports a family.
- Named by Steven or Joshua: yes, Joshua (06 B0007), on nightly out-of-body travel. Steven never names Monroe.
- Standing and criticism: first-person testimony, never independently verified; tests of the Monroe Institute's Hemi-Sync audio are mixed (Wikipedia). The remote-viewing schools Steven trained in inherit its vocabulary.

### monroe1994
- Citation: Robert A. Monroe, Ultimate Journey. New York: Doubleday, 1994.
- Confirmed at: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Monroe; text read at https://media.zoraweb.com/files/zw/books/ultimate_journey-Monroe.pdf (ch. 12 and after).
- What it reports: Beliefs must become "Knowns" through one's own experience. The guides withhold: "We cannot give you that answer" and "Go find out—that's what you should do." Monroe concludes the guide was part of his own "I-There": "when you talk to yourself there is no barrier of any kind!"

- Named by Steven or Joshua: as above.
- Standing and criticism: as above.

### hernandez2018
- Citation: Reinerio Hernandez, Robert Davis, Russell Scalpone and Rudolph Schild, "A Study on Reported Contact with Non-Human Intelligence Associated with Unidentified Aerial Phenomena," Journal of Scientific Exploration 32(2): 298–348, 2018.
- Confirmed at: https://journalofscientificexploration.org/index.php/jse/article/view/1282/801 (DOI 10.31275/2018.1282).
- What it reports: Two anonymous online surveys (N = 3,256; 1,919 in phase two). About 70% said contact changed their life positively. Most reported no abduction. Of those who received communication, 67% reported reassuring messages, 59% a message "of Love or of Oneness", 37% two-way telepathy, and 34% a thought or voice "downloaded" with no being present.
- Parallel: 01 B0269; 02 B0128 "a black space"; 01 B0279 "I get info dumped". Closeness: close in kind. Difference: tick-box aggregates, nothing like Steven's sustained relationships.
- Named by Steven or Joshua: no.
- Standing and criticism: self-selected respondents recruited through the foundation's site; no control group (the authors call for one); a journal devoted to anomalies; not replicated. Ellen Tarr's objections: https://drmsh.com/dr-ellen-tarr-has-some-good-questions-for-the-free-ufo-experiencer-survey-folks/ (not read in full).

### powers2017
- Citation: Albert R. Powers III, Megan S. Kelley and Philip R. Corlett, "Varieties of Voice-Hearing: Psychics and the Psychosis Continuum," Schizophrenia Bulletin 43(1): 84–98, 2017.
- Confirmed at: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5216860
- What it reports: Four groups (N = 67), including 17 clairaudient psychics and 16 voice-hearing patients. The voices were alike in sound, content and frequency. The psychics had more control (often learned by practice), less distress, kinder voices and a warmer reception when they first told someone.
- Parallel: 07 B0048, B0051; 01 B0168 "very loving and strange, but not bad"; the psychiatrist's "The worst thing you could do is suppress this" (01 B0173). Closeness: close. Difference: age of onset; Steven's control is "a work in progress" (07 B0019).
- Named by Steven or Joshua: no.
- Standing and criticism: small sample; the authors note selection bias, demand effects and unknown long-term stability.

### foxwell2020
- Citation: John Foxwell, Ben Alderson-Day, Charles Fernyhough and Angela Woods, "'I've learned I need to treat my characters like people'," Consciousness and Cognition 79: 102901, 2020. With: Marjorie Taylor, Sara Hodges and Adèle Kohányi, "The illusion of independent agency," Imagination, Cognition and Personality 22(4): 361–380, 2003.
- Confirmed at: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7068700/ ; https://bpb-us-e1.wpmucdn.com/blogs.uoregon.edu/dist/4/2521/files/2013/03/Taylor-Hodges-Kohanyi_2003-2b6wdel.pdf
- What it reports: Of 181 writers, 63% hear their characters, 61% say characters act independently, 15% converse with them as themselves; 21% had hallucination-like experiences, mostly while falling asleep or waking.
- Parallel: 01 B0267; 03 B0027; 07 B0052 "You have to treat this as if it is happening"; 07 B0099 "the puppet will eventually become more and more representative". Closeness: close. Difference: writers know they began the character; Steven's figures arrive uninvited and remember across weeks (02 B0098).
- Named by Steven or Joshua: not the studies; both make the analogy.
- Standing and criticism: survey self-report, consistent across two studies. It shows autonomy can come from practised imagination, not that it always does.

### luhrmann2012
- Citation: T. M. Luhrmann, When God Talks Back. New York: Knopf, 2012. With: Luhrmann, Howard Nusbaum and Ronald Thisted, "The Absorption Hypothesis," American Anthropologist, 2010.

- Parallel: 01 B0258 "the more I entertain it, the more real it becomes"; 07 B0071. Closeness: close on method. Difference: a church teaches the evangelicals; Steven says "I received basically no formal instruction" (07 B0002).
- Named by Steven or Joshua: no.
- Standing and criticism: well regarded in anthropology; later replications UNVERIFIED here.

### swedenborg1758
- Citation: Emanuel Swedenborg, Heaven and Hell, 1758; trans. John C. Ager, §§246–256.
- Confirmed at: https://eswedenborg.com/writings/static/d5399/246.htm
- What it reports: Spirits who speak with a person use that person's language and memory; §256 adds that a spirit may not speak from its own memory, or the person would take its thoughts for his own.
- Parallel: 07 B0016 "all of it uses your hardware"; 07 B0038, others' thoughts "feel like your thoughts". Closeness: close. Difference: Swedenborg treats the channel as accurate, Steven as heavily distorted.
- Named by Steven or Joshua: no.
- Standing and criticism: a religious classic with no evidential standing. Swedenborg, like Steven, was a technical man (a mining official) who changed course in mid-life (biography from general knowledge, not re-verified).

### corbin1964
- Citation: Henry Corbin, "Mundus Imaginalis, or the Imaginary and the Imaginal" (1964); English in Spring, 1972.
- Confirmed at: https://www.australiansuficentre.org/sufi_teachings_mundus_imaginalis.htm ; https://swedenborg.com/scholars-mundus-imaginalis-ii-the-spiritual-imagination/
- What it reports: Reading Persian philosophers, Corbin describes an intermediate world (ʿālam al-mithāl) between sense and intellect, objectively real and perceived by imagination as an organ of knowledge. He coined "imaginal" because "imaginary" had come to mean unreal. He adds that the "most exact formulation of all this" in the West "is found perhaps in Swedenborg."
- Parallel: 07 B0061; 07 B0063 "It's the maybe zone"; 01 B0274 "more than imagination, but less than a physical reality". Closeness: close in concept. Difference: Corbin's world belongs to a theistic order; Steven's is a private "working space" others can write into.
- Named by Steven or Joshua: no; the word is Joshua's, then Steven's.
- Standing and criticism: a philosophical and historical thesis, not an empirical claim.

### jung1962
- Citation: C. G. Jung, Memories, Dreams, Reflections, ed. Aniela Jaffé (English 1963); The Red Book: Liber Novus, ed. Sonu Shamdasani. New York: W. W. Norton, 2009.
- Confirmed at: https://www.taylorfrancis.com/books/9781134699902/chapters/10.4324/9780203360590-14 ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Red_Book_(Jung)
- What it reports: From 1913 Jung deliberately evoked fantasies while awake and entered them. A recurring teacher, Philemon, convinced him the psyche holds things he did not make. By Barbara Hannah's report he never let a figure leave until it had said why it came.
- Parallel: 01 B0258; 07 B0052; a teaching figure with a "curriculum" (02 B0139). Closeness: moderate. Difference: Jung keeps the figures within the psyche; his method is solitary and waking, not hypnagogic.
- Named by Steven or Joshua: no.
- Standing and criticism: a clinical and literary tradition, not experimental evidence.

### stang2016
- Citation: Charles M. Stang, Our Divine Double. Cambridge, MA: Harvard University Press, 2016.
- Confirmed at: https://bmcr.brynmawr.edu/?p=35374 (review by Gregory Shaw).
- What it reports: An ancient idea that each person has a divine counterpart or twin: Socrates' daimonion, the Gospel of Thomas, Plotinus, and above all Mani, whose heavenly Twin came at twelve and again at twenty-four. Socrates on his voice: "it always holds me back from what I am thinking of doing, but never urges me forward" (Apology 31d, https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Perseus%3Atext%3A1999.01.0170%3Atext%3DApol.%3Asection%3D31d).
- Parallel: 01 B0277 "You can imagine a twin that wasn't born. Just chose not to be born"; 02 B0031 "she looks a lot like me"; Joshua's "your double" (01 B0249). Closeness: moderate. Difference: the ancient twin is one's own higher self and brings the revelation; Iris is a sibling, and the teaching comes from Mother.
- Named by Steven or Joshua: no.
- Standing and criticism: respected history of religions. Two looser relatives: the Zoroastrian daēnā, a maiden who meets the soul after death as the image of its deeds (https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/den), and the guardian angel: "Beside each believer stands an angel as protector and shepherd leading him to life" (Catechism §336, https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/__P1A.HTM); compare 01 B0307.

## What is distinctive in Steven's account

- **A soul mother who is not God.** Mother "created our souls" (02 B0136), yet she "asked for a miracle" and was surprised to get twins (03 B0166, B0208). I found creators, caretakers (Newton) and larger selves (Seth, Monroe), but no finite, praying parent of souls. The Latter-day Saint Heavenly Mother is the nearest tradition I know (UNVERIFIED).
- **The twin as an unborn sister.** She bakes a cake, says "That's too big", then cuts herself an enormous slice (02 B0045–B0050). The divine-double tradition has nothing this domestic.
- **An engineer's error model.** Clear contact is "one in fifty" (07 B0098); most is self-talk (07 B0099); channels are ranked by reliability (07 B0080–B0085); the remedy is "error correction" (07 B0072). Channelling literature rarely measures its own noise.
- **Disappointment.** Shown that he is everything, he is disappointed and lonely (02 B0172, B0182), then chooses that it matters. Non-dual reports usually speak of love or bliss.
- **Volunteered counter-explanations.** "I think I'm also looking at artifacts in my cornea" (01 B0235); of the mediums, "No dead ringers or anything" (01 B0313).
- **Fictional templates named openly.** The white space is "a lot like *The Matrix*" (02 B0026), and he cites Dumbledore at King's Cross: "Of course it is happening inside your head, Harry, but why on earth should that mean that it is not real?" (https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Harry_Potter_and_the_Deathly_Hallows). Both scenes are white, empty places.

## What a careful skeptic would say

1. **The states do the work.** Sleep onset, waking at 2 a.m., and long still meditation in a dark closet are the conditions in which healthy people see autonomous images, hear speech and feel presences (Mavromatis; Lindahl; Foxwell). Steven also tried galantamine (04 B0150), a lucid-dream aid (LaBerge and colleagues, PLOS ONE, 2018; findings UNVERIFIED).
2. **The relationship was cultivated.** He began by pretending, and "the more I entertain it, the more real it becomes" (01 B0258). Evangelicals, tulpa-makers and novelists report the same result from sustained, serious make-believe. No illness is implied and no outside agent is needed.
3. **The content has a library.** He read Seth and the Law of One, took King's course, paid for channelled readings, and trained among people who inherit Monroe's vocabulary. "I had not done a lot of study on esoteric stuff" (02 B0082) should sit beside 07 B0046.
4. **What is easy is uncheckable; what is checkable is hard.** Names and exact answers resist (02 B0066; 07 B0090–B0092); feelings flow. When Mother says "Open your eyes" he sees nothing (03 B0192). The hits (01 B0294, 01 B0322, 03 B0216) were not recorded under conditions that exclude memory, coincidence or ordinary channels, and misses are absorbed: "you'll be wrong in a way that teaches you something about yourself" (03 B0243).
5. **Meaning is assigned.** Of coincidences: "It's always up to you to decide what they mean" (07 B0010).
6. **It does not look like illness.** He sought assessment, functioned, felt better, and the contact faded under stress (02 B0092). Powers and colleagues show such people exist. But "a little late" for schizophrenia at 36 (02 B0091) overstates a tendency (epidemiology UNVERIFIED).
7. **The demonstrations are informal.** The live pen test was mixed (03 B0338–B0361), and Gardner showed how blindfold claims fail under strict control.

None of this shows the figures were not who Steven took them to be. It shows the same experiences can arise without them.
